1.  Title The name of the project.

    -   Unemployment in the EU.

2.  Description Short explanation: what does the project do and why is
    it useful or interesting?

    -   This project analyzes unemployment trends and GDP growth in
        European Union (EU) countries from 2013 to 2024.

    -   It cleans and processes raw data from various sources,
        calculates key indicators, and visualizes the relationship
        between GDP growth and unemployment (overall and by gender). The
        project also generates a geographical map of average
        unemployment across Europe.

    -   It is useful for researchers, economists, students, and
        policymakers to better understand economic development patterns
        and the impact of events like COVID-19 on employment.

6.  Features (optional) What are the most important functions or
    capabilities?

    -   Cleans and preprocesses multiple EU datasets (GDP, unemployment
        by gender, population)

    -   Calculates GDP growth pre- and post-COVID

    -   Calculates unemployment percentages relative to total workforce

    -   Produces visualizations:

        -   Cumulative GDP index

        -   Unemployment by gender (boxplot)

        -   GDP vs Unemployment (dual y-axis plot)

        -   Map of average unemployment rates.
